DayDial turns your daily schedule into a visual clock face. Events appear as colorful arcs around a 24-hour dial — drag to move, pinch to resize, and plan your day in seconds.

★ RADIAL CLOCK FACE
Your entire day on one screen. No endless lists, no cramped grids — just a beautiful analog clock where every event is an arc.

★ MULTI-LAYER LANES
Up to 4 concentric layers handle overlapping events gracefully. No more guessing what conflicts with what.

★ RECURRING EVENTS
Daily, weekdays, weekly, and monthly recurrence with per-occurrence overrides. Change one instance without breaking the pattern.

★ REMINDERS & PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
Never miss an event. FCM push notifications for authenticated users, local notifications for guests.

★ iCAL IMPORT / EXPORT
Full RFC 5545 compatible .ics support. Import from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. Export your schedule anywhere.
